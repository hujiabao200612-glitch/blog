abstract_en: "Recursive self-improvement (RSI) aims to transform interaction experience into persistent improvements, yet external feedback is costly and world-model predictions may become unreliable as policies evolve. We introduce RSI-Anything, a domain-general framework built on JEPA-Anything that uses predictive world models to guide action and learning-data selection. Observed feedback drives policy or workflow improvement and conditional world-model adaptation, while component and compatibility checks govern the retention of updated policy-model pairs. Across diverse computational and scientific domains, RSI-Anything demonstrates substantial gains. In code repair, with the coder fixed, success increases from 26.3% with a static verifier to 29.2% through predictive selection and 31.2% after world-model adaptation, at comparable test usage. Graphical user interface (GUI) evaluations showed higher task completion, while average actions decrease by 39.4%. Across five successive policy versions, laboratory-planning scores more than double from 21.80% to 46.01%. In reaction optimization, RSI-Anything achieves 96.15% mean best observed yield versus 82.16% for Bayesian optimization under the same 40-experiment budget. An end-to-end immune-science application further connects workflow evolution and predictive modeling with therapeutic hypothesis generation. These results demonstrate how predictive world models and retained system updates enable recursive improvement across diverse environments."

## 中文解读

这篇论文解决的是递归自我改进（RSI）里的一个核心矛盾：想让智能体从交互经验中持续变强，但外部反馈（人工标注、真实环境试错）太贵；而如果完全靠内部的世界模型做预测，策略一变、分布一漂，预测又不可靠了。

RSI-Anything 的思路是"预测 + 保留"双循环：

1. **预测性世界模型做向导**：基于 JEPA-Anything 的世界模型，同时指导"下一步做什么动作"和"哪些交互数据值得学"；
2. **观测反馈驱动双更新**：真实反馈既改进策略/工作流，也做世界模型的条件性适配；
3. **组件与兼容性检查做守门**：只有通过检查的"策略-模型"组合才会被保留下来，避免改进退化。

实验覆盖面很广——代码修复、GUI 操作、实验室规划、化学反应优化、免疫科学，说明这是一个领域通用的框架，而不是只为某个任务定制的技巧。最有说服力的是实验室规划那组数字：五个版本迭代，得分从 21.8% 翻到 46%，这是"递归"二字的直接证据。
