As these agents increasingly drive AI research and practical applications, their design, evaluation, and continuous improvement present intricate, multifaceted challenges. This book provides a comprehensive overview, framing intelligent agents within modular, brain-inspired architectures that integrate principles from cognitive science, neuroscience, and computational research. We structure our exploration into four interconnected parts. First, we systematically investigate the modular foundation of intelligent agents, systematically mapping their cognitive, perceptual, and operational modules onto analogous human brain functionalities and elucidating core components such as memory, world modeling, reward processing, goal, and emotion. Second, we discuss self-enhancement and adaptive evolution mechanisms, exploring how agents autonomously refine their capabilities, adapt to dynamic environments, and achieve continual learning through automated optimization paradigms. Third, we examine multi-agent systems, investigating the collective intelligence emerging from agent interactions, cooperation, and societal structures. ## 中文解读

这是四篇里视野最宏大的一篇（48 位作者的"大一统"综述），适合当"全景底图"来读。

它的组织逻辑是脑启发的模块化架构：
1. **模块化基础**：感知、记忆、世界模型、奖励、目标、情感——每个模块都能在人脑找到对应物；
2. **自我增强与演化**：智能体如何自主变强（连回自进化主题）；
3. **多智能体与群体智能**：协作、竞争、社会结构；
4. **安全**：内外部威胁、对齐、鲁棒性。

四篇的阅读关系可以这样排：这篇搭全景底图 →《A Survey of Self-Evolving Agents》深入"自进化"这一支 →《Agents of Change》看一个具体系统的实现 →《Agent Skills》理解能力沉淀的形式。四篇合起来，智能体方向的核心问题基本就覆盖了。
